Simulated trades

A paper trader runs the same rules on every rating in the background. No real coins are involved; it shows whether a simple buy-the-dip rule would have made money after EA's 5% tax.

Trading rules
  • Fair price is the median of every reading in the previous 7 days (needs at least 6 readings).
  • Buy when the price is 10% or more below fair, spending up to 50,000 coins on that rating.
  • Sell when selling would make 6% or more after the 5% tax, or after 7 days, or if the price falls 20% below cost.
  • One open position per rating at a time.
